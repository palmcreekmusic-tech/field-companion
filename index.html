how to use this

  1. Tap a key on the OP–1 field to see what it does. Tap the same key again to zoom back out.
  2. Combinations: tap shift, then another key. The screen changes the same way the real unit does.
  3. Ask anything in the box under the device. You get short steps, and show me plays them on the device one key at a time.
  4. Sounds: ask for a random preset, something fun to try, or an effect with knob positions. You can shuffle it, keep the parts you like, and save it in the sound lab.
  5. Play mode: switch learn to play under the device and it becomes a little instrument. Tap or slide across the keys (or use A, W, S, E, D… on a computer), drag the colored knobs to shape the sound, and use ● ▶ ■ to record a two-bar loop on four tracks. Recipe cards have hear a sketch and play it on the keys. The sounds are made in your browser, so they only resemble the real engines.
  6. Knobs turn: drag up or down on any colored knob, or scroll over it. In learn mode the screen tells you what that knob does on the current page.
  7. Today's challenge changes every day. It gives you one small thing to try, with the steps shown on the device.
  8. While steps play, use back, next, the step dots or speed (slow, normal, fast) to go at your own pace.
  9. Sounds: keys click softly and zooming makes a gentle whoosh, and the fm radio hisses softly for a few seconds when it comes up on screen or in an answer (tuning brings it back). Turn them off with the speaker button at the top right.
  10. Done with something? Press ✓ done at the top (or Esc) to clear the answers and go back to just the device and the ask box. Your questions stay in past questions.
  11. Orange dots on keys mean there's something new for that key that you taught it or the web found.
  12. Teach it your own tips from the menu. It uses them in every answer after that.
